Legends for the five main-text figures of `manuscript_v57/scJDO_v57_draft4.md`.

## Fig 1 — Lyapunov gauge, price schedule, and cross-comparison

**A.** Schematic of the Lyapunov-gauge decomposition of the drift Jacobian: `J(x) = (−D/2 + A) Σ^{−1}` with drift `f(x) = −(D/2 − A) ∇V(x)` and noise covariance `D`. Colour-coded: symmetric part (blue) — a covariance-derived object, identifiable from the snapshot; antisymmetric part `A` (red) — not identified from the snapshot, encodes flow direction, oscillation phase and rotational structure.

**B.** Price of the antisymmetric part: schematic bars showing which experimental augmentation recovers which class of output. Left to right, top to bottom: `A(x)` for any point requires `d − 1` static perturbations of independent inputs; the sign of rotation requires one time-resolved perturbation; per-cell velocity is recovered by metabolic labelling (provided the reference is not a linear function of the input representation, see §2.5); lineage displacement is recovered by clonal barcoding. Bars are schematic (not scaled to experimental cost).

**C.** Estimator cross-comparison on the marrow Ery calibration substrate — six real fits (three folds × two seeds). Whitened symmetric part of scJDO's Jacobian shows partial agreement at the matrix level with the Lyapunov-gauge prediction `−½ Σ^{−1/2} D̂ Σ^{−1/2}` (leading-eigenvector `|cos|` 0.33 ± 0.15; Frobenius correlation 0.46 ± 0.01, mean ± SD across the six fits) and is better than the local-precision baseline (`|cos|` 0.28 ± 0.07; Frobenius correlation −0.38 ± 0.00) — the leading direction is not reliably recovered, but the matrix-level agreement beats the naive alternative [ EL03 ].

## Fig 2 — Estimator fidelity and crossing-modality limit on synthetic ground truth

Panels regenerated by `manuscript_v57/regen_fig2_synthetic.py` from `reproducibility/blind_saddle_benchmark/outputs/V2FIXED_SUMMARY.json` (Panel A/B) and the r11 learned-vs-oracle argmax data (Panel C; see §2.2 and the panel-C data-source note in the regen script).

**A. Oracle-at-fixed-point crossing vs `τ_crit`.** For the fixed-design two-branch pitchfork V2-fixed benchmark, the analytic Jacobian evaluated at the symmetric fixed point crosses zero at the pre-declared `±0.05` tolerance for every one of the six conditions (`τ_crit ∈ {0.1, 0.3, 0.5}`, two seeds each). Points are (`τ_crit`, learned crossing of `λ_max` at the fixed point); annotations show signed error from `τ_crit`; the shaded band is the ±0.05 tolerance envelope; the dashed line is `y = x` [ EL08 ].

**B. Oracle-at-cells and learned-at-cells traces on the interior grid.** On the same conditions, the analytic Jacobian evaluated at sampled cell states (green) and scJDO's per-cell aggregation of the learned Jacobian (red) both remain positive across the entire interior pseudotime grid — neither crosses zero. Scorecard: oracle-at-cells 0/6, learned-at-cells 0/6. Vertical dashed line marks an example `τ_crit`; the horizontal dashed line is zero. This is the modality limit: on a two-branch pitchfork substrate, no cell-evaluated Jacobian localises the bifurcation [ EL08 ].

**C. Learned-vs-oracle argmax agreement across 3 seeds (interior toggle-SDE benchmark).** On the interior toggle-SDE estimator-fidelity benchmark (`α_min = 0.5` interior sweep, kernel bandwidth `h = 0.02`, 3 fitting seeds; Methods, supplement S3), per-seed argmax of the learned per-cell aggregation `⟨Re λ_max(J_i)⟩_τ` is plotted against the corresponding oracle argmax. Points fall within 0.01–0.09 of the `y = x` diagonal — the learned per-cell aggregation reproduces the oracle argmax at the estimator-fidelity level [ EL07 ].

(Round A per-cell aggregation temporal reproducibility on real marrow Ery — peak-τ 0.020 ± 0.000 across three fitting seeds; pairwise curve Pearson 0.806 — is described in the Fig 3 legend below, not here, as it is a real-data reproducibility observation with a boundary-peak location rather than an estimator-fidelity readout.)

## Fig 3 — Real-data stability on marrow Ery

**A.** Fraction of matched signed cosines ≥ 0.85 (Hungarian one-to-one matching to per-fold reference) for REAL fits (6 fits, 3 folds × 2 seeds), block-permutation null (30 fits, 10 draws × 3 folds), and circular-shift null (same). Both nulls yield HIGHER stability fractions than REAL because kernel-averaging over a marginally-preserved pseudotime distribution collapses to a trivially consistent operator (diagnostic; see §2.3 text) [ EL15 ].

**B.** Held-out reconstruction gain(K) as a function of dictionary size K on the discovery fold, evaluated on the two validation folds' Jacobian tensors via non-negative least-squares activations. Blue: real gain per K. Orange dashed: block-null 95th percentile per K. Only K = 1 exceeds the null band (real 0.938 vs null 0.937). K_eff = 1 [ EL15 ].

**C. Whitened symmetric-part agreement — six real fits.** Whitened symmetric part of scJDO's Jacobian, aggregated across all six real fits (3 folds × 2 seeds), compared to the Lyapunov-gauge prediction `−½ Σ^{−1/2} D̂ Σ^{−1/2}` (left pair) and to the local-precision baseline `Σ^{−1}` (right pair). Values are mean ± SD across the six fits: leading-eigenvector `|cos|` 0.33 ± 0.15 (Lyapunov) vs 0.28 ± 0.07 (precision); mean Frobenius correlation 0.46 ± 0.01 (Lyapunov) vs −0.38 ± 0.00 (precision). Partial agreement at the matrix level; leading direction not reliably recovered; better than the precision baseline [ EL03 ].

**D. Real-data reproducibility of the eigenvalue curve.** Per-cell aggregation of the learned Jacobian yields a `Re λ_max(τ)` curve whose peak sits at the pseudotime boundary (peak-τ 0.020 ± 0.000 across 3 fitting seeds; pairwise full-curve Pearson 0.806). The peak-location reproducibility is high but the peak is at the boundary of the pseudotime range, not at an interior maximum — an observation to be read alongside §2.2's cell-vs-fixed-point crossing scorecard when the peak location is used to argue for a regime transition [ EL09 ].

## Fig 4 — LARRY lineage ground truth (two cohorts)

**A.** Day-2 fate prediction (Neu vs Mono of the eventual clone) — mean AUROC and error bars per feature set, side by side for the two cohorts. Left group: 154-cell / 106-clone Gate 1 v2 cohort (compute-driven subsample; 5-fold clone-grouped CV × 3 CV seeds) [ EL10 ]. Right group: full eligible cohort (970 cells / 632 clones; scJDO fit substrate 4,638 barcoded + 9,362 unbarcoded fill = 14,000; E baselines on all 28,249 day-2 cells; 5-fold clone-grouped CV × 5 CV seeds) [ EL10b ]. Feature sets: E_PCA (30 principal components), E_FA (30 factor-analysis components), E_scVI (30-dim scVI latent), S_FA (per-cell scJDO features), E_FA + S_FA. Full-cohort AUROCs: E_PCA 0.867, E_FA 0.864, E_scVI 0.879, S_FA 0.554, E_FA + S_FA 0.863. **Stated primary contrast**: ΔAUROC(E_FA + S_FA − E_FA) = −0.001 with 95 % CI [−0.002, +0.000] — adding scJDO on top of the FA baseline does not improve fate prediction; secondary S_FA − E_scVI = −0.325 with 95 % CI [−0.340, −0.309]. The negative direction of Gate 1 v2 is preserved on the full eligible cohort.

**B.** Gate 3 strict — soft-mode falsification on 520 LARRY clones with ≥ 5 mature descendants. Arm 1 (FA30 + N) AUROC 0.646; Arms 2–4 add local covariance features (C, top-3 eigvals of Σ_local + softness ratio) and Mojtahedi Ic [11]. All arms are indistinguishable; 95 % paired-bootstrap CIs on ΔAUROC vs Arm 1 include zero [ EL11 ].

**C.** Sanity: per-day-2-cell softness ratio scattered against day-2 Palantir pseudotime [3]. Spearman correlation ≈ +0.47; features track pseudotime but are redundant with expression at the clone level. Confirms failure in Panels A/B is not a broken pipeline.

## Fig 5 — Labelling comparison on scNT-seq KCl-stimulated neurons + synthetic circular substrate

Fig 5 is a three-panel figure (A / B / C); the "interpretive summary" that appeared as a fourth panel in draft 1 has been moved into §2.5 body text.

**A.** Temporal contrast `‖J(τ) − J̄‖_F / ‖J̄‖_F` per grid point on the scNT-seq KCl-stimulated cortical-neuron cohort [2] (3,060 cells; time-since-KCl 0–120 min), for R (labelling reference — nascent-transcript matrix projected onto the PCA basis, `R = log1p(M_n_hvg) @ PCs_hvg`, black), G (geometry-only scJDO, dashed blue), L (velocity-matching scJDO, dashed red). L median 0.532 vs R 0.234 (ratio 2.27) — L is more time-varying than R itself. Across-seed spread of L (0.011) is half of G's (0.021) [ EL06 ].

**B.** Per-window absolute cosine between L's leading Jacobian eigenvector and R's leading eigenvector, averaged over 3 L seeds. Median over τ = 0.974; L's leading direction agrees with R per-window [ EL06 ]. Caveat: R on this cohort is a linear projection into the same PCA basis as X_pca, so per-window agreement between L and R does not imply that L recovers a per-cell velocity outside the span of X_pca — a circular-agreement caveat, not an independent recovery of the reference (see §2.5, §3 Discussion) [ EL13 ].

**C.** Synthetic circular substrate — reversal test. Antisymmetric-Jacobian component projected on the cycle plane (A_12), median over τ, per arm on the synthetic 30-D circular substrate (N = 1,500 cells; substrate generator seed 42). At `vel_scale = 0`, both forward-τ and reverse-τ arms yield magnitudes ≤ 0.05 (grey bars, no reversal) [ EL14 ]. At `vel_scale = 2`, forward-τ yields +0.149 and reverse-τ yields −0.204 (red / blue bars, clean sign flip) [ EL05 ]. Error bars = across-3-seed spread. Rotation direction on this synthetic circular substrate is prior-selected by the `vel_scale` slot, not snapshot-determined.
